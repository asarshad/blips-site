Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 23, 2026

Blips (“we”, “us”) is operated by Husni Consulting Inc. and provides a mobile news feed app that surfaces articles, videos, and short reels from third‑party sources. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Summary

  • We aim to collect as little personal data as possible.
  • We use data to operate the app, improve reliability, and prevent abuse.
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data by emailing info@husniconsulting.ca.

Information we may collect

Depending on features you use, we may collect:

  • App usage and diagnostics (e.g., crash reports, performance metrics) to improve stability.
  • Device and technical data (e.g., OS version, app version) for troubleshooting.
  • Account information only if you choose to create an account (e.g., email), to provide that service.

Content and third‑party links

Blips links to content hosted by third parties (news sites, YouTube, etc.). When you open those links, their privacy policies apply.

How we use information

  • Provide core app functionality and customer support.
  • Monitor and improve app performance and reliability.
  • Prevent abuse and protect the service.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your choices

  • Access / deletion: email info@husniconsulting.ca.
  • Opt‑out: you may disable certain permissions or uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions? Contact info@husniconsulting.ca.

Note: This policy is a starting point and may need adjustments based on the exact analytics, authentication, and third‑party services you enable in production.
